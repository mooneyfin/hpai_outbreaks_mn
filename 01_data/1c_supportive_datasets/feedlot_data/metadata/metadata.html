Section 1: Overview

Originator:Minnesota Pollution Control Agency

Title: Feedlots in Minnesota

Abstract: This dataset contains point representations of the locations of animal feedlot facilities in Minnesota.The data includes contact information, registration/permit information, animal counts, animal units, and information about nearby water bodies.

Purpose: The locations of animal feedlots provide information and context to support decisions on animal health, land use, and maintaining environmental quality.

category_fdlt
-
High-level categorization for this feedlot. Is it permitted, registered, not required to register, or inactive?
type_fdlt
-
Mid-level categorization for this feedlot. Combines the feedlot category and, if registration is required, whether or not their registration is current.
reg_required
-
Does the MPCA require this feedlot to register under Minnesota Rules Chapter 7020? A feedlot is required to register if it has at least 10 animal units in shoreland, or at least 50 animal units outside of shoreland. Some of MPCA's county partners also enter feedlots into our database that are not required to register by state rules, so there may be feedlots in this dataset that were not required to register with the MPCA.
reg_current
-
Is the feedlot's registration up-to-date? Registration windows start every four years, and feedlots must register at least once within each window. The current window is 1/1/2018-12/31/2021 and the previous window was 1/1/2014 to 12/31/2017. Registrations are considered up-to-date if they happened on 1/1/2014 or later because there is still time to register in the current window.
per_class
-
Class of the most recent permit
per_active
-
Is there an effective permit at this feedlot?
npdes_sds
-
Is the permit a NPDES permit or an SDS permit? NPDES is National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System - in other words, a permit issued under the federal Clean Water Act. SDS is State Disposal System - a permit issued under Minnesota statutes. A permit may be issued under both NPDES and SDS.
